THE PHILOSOPHY
Confidently Boring
Everyone's selling hype. You're selling compound growth. The boring angle is incredibly differentiated.
If you've spent any time using AI for marketing, you've probably noticed something frustrating: Everyone has
the same tools. Everyone's reading the same prompting guides. And everyone's output sounds... the same.
Generic headlines. Corporate-speak copy. Email sequences that read like they were written by a committee of
robots who've never actually sold anything.
You've tried better prompts. You've tried longer prompts. You've tried prompt chains and templates and "the
ultimate marketing prompt pack."

Here's what I figured out after 18 months of building marketing systems with AI: The problem isn't your

RESEARCH → FOUNDATION → STRUCTURE → ASSETS → ITERATION
STAGE 01
=== PAGE 9 ===
STAGE 01
Research: Deep Context
Before we write a single word, we understand the landscape. MCPs give Claude real-time access to

Market research — Who are the competitors? What are they saying? Where are the gaps?

FRAMEWORK 04
Decision Framework
You have research but too many options. Here are 6 methods for choosing.
METHOD 01
Specialized Agents
Spin up 3-5 expert agents → Each analyzes independently → Where do they overlap? That's your answer.
When: You have research but too many options Job: Get objective validation from multiple perspectives
METHOD 02
AI Ping Pong
Claude 4.5 Opus (create) → Gemini 3 Pro (critique) → Claude 4.5 Opus (refine) → GPT-5.2 (validate) →
Perplexity (fact-check)
When: High-stakes decisions need external validation Job: Break the echo chamber, stress-test ideas
METHOD 03
Recommendation Request
"We have this research. Here's the goal. What do you recommend?"
When: Overwhelmed by options, need AI to synthesize Job: Get clear recommendation with reasoning &
trade-offs
METHOD 04
Constraint Filter
Impact vs Effort → Speed to value → Resource requirements → Risk level
=== PAGE 11 ===
When: Too many options pass initial filter Job: Prioritize ruthlessly by what's doable NOW
METHOD 05
Market Validation
What are competitors doing? → What's working? → Where are gaps? → Which option fills biggest gap?
When: Choosing positioning, messaging, or product direction Job: Validate against real market data
METHOD 06
Future Self Check
Imagine 12 months from now → We chose Option A → What does business look like? → Repeat for Option B
→ Compare
When: Strategic decisions with long-term impact Job: Project outcomes to see which future looks better

SKILL DEEP DIVE: POSITIONING ANGLES
The same product can sell 100x better with a different angle. Not a different product. Not better features.
Just a different way of framing what it already does.
The 4-Step Process
1. Identify the transformation — Not what you sell, what life looks like AFTER
2. Map the competitive landscape — What alternatives exist?
3. Find the unique mechanism — HOW you deliver results differently
4. Assess market sophistication — Schwartz's 5 stages determine which angle type works
The 8 Angle Types
The Contrarian — Challenge what everyone believes
The Unique Mechanism — Lead with the HOW, not just WHAT
The Transformation — Before and after, the gap closed
The Enemy — Position against a common villain
The Speed/Ease — Compress time or reduce effort
The Specificity — Hyper-specific about who and what
The Social Proof — Lead with evidence, not claims
The Risk Reversal — Make the guarantee the headline
Output: 3-5 distinct angles with psychology, headline directions, and when to use each.
